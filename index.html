ABOUT THE STORY

A Promise, a Journey, and a Second Chance

After failing to control his anger, King Wawigan breaks his promise. Disappointed by his actions, Princess Saripu takes Prince Wulan back to the heavenly realm.

Determined to make up for his mistake, the king faces various difficult trials in the heavenly realm with the help of Garuda and a kind-hearted eel.

Will King Wawigan reunite with his family? Discover a meaningful tale about the importance of keeping promises and having the courage to admit one's mistakes.

NEED HELP?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install an application?

No. The AR experience runs directly through your web browser.

Why is my camera not opening?

Make sure camera access is allowed in your browser settings and the website is opened through HTTPS.

Why is the page not being detected?

Make sure the book is placed under good lighting and the entire illustration is visible to the camera.

Can I explore the scenes without the book?

Yes. Select any scene from the 3D section to explore it without scanning the book.

Why is there no sound?

Audio narration is currently under development. Once available, check your device volume and audio playback settings.
